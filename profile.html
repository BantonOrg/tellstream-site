🛡️ Privacy Policy

1. Data Minimization & Privacy
Tellstream is a private community lounge. We do not track, profile, analyze, or sell your data. No advertising trackers are loaded on this platform.

2. Browser Local Storage
We use browser local storage purely for functional features: remembering your chat handle, game session tokens, and player volume slider state.

3. Chat Visibility & Plaintext Storage
Please note that this is a public chat lounge. Messages are transmitted and stored in plaintext in the database so that chat history can load when the page is refreshed. They are visible to database administrators for moderation. Do not type any personal details, passwords, or private information in public chat channels.

4. Passkey Hash Security
If you choose to lock your handle, your passkey is immediately hashed server-side using secure database procedures. Raw passkeys are never stored in plaintext and cannot be read by anyone.

🍪 We use functional local storage to remember your nickname, volume, and active games. No tracking or ads. Read Privacy Policy
