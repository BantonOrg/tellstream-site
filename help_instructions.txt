================================================================================
                           TELLSTREAM HELP INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

This document contains simple, plain-English instructions and console commands 
for Tellstream. They are divided into four sections: General, Registered Member, 
DJ / Presenter, and Station Admin.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS (Lounge Chat & Noticeboard)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Choose a Chat Name:
  Before you start chatting, enter your name in the "Chat Name" box at the bottom 
  right of the sidebar. This sets a temporary identity for your current session. 
  If you close or reload the page, the name can be claimed by anyone else unless 
  you lock it permanently.

* Sending Messages:
  Simply type your message in the chat input box at the bottom of the screen and 
  press "Enter" on your keyboard (or click the "Send" button).

* Emojis & Smilies:
  Click any emoji shortcut below the chat input to insert it into your message. 
  Click the [See All Codes] link to open the full library of custom smilies.

* Strikes & Lockout Rules:
  Bad language is blocked automatically and tracked via a strike system:
  - 3 Strikes: Temporary 24-hour lockout from the chat.
  - 4+ Strikes: Permanent chat ban.
  - Apologizing: Type the word "sorry" in the main chat to remove 1 strike 
    (limited to once per calendar day).

* Noticeboard View Rules:
  When you open the Noticeboard, the lounge chat is completely hidden. You must 
  close the Noticeboard using the "Exit Noticeboard" button to return to the 
  active chat stream.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. REGISTERED MEMBER INSTRUCTIONS (Level 0+)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Locking & Securing Your Username:
  Click the padlock button next to your chat name to open your profile settings. 
  Enter a secret Passkey, a key reminder hint, and a recovery Email. Once saved, 
  no one else can use your username without your passkey.

* Customizing Your Profile Card:
  In your profile settings, you can upload a profile picture (under 500KB), share 
  your location or social media links, and write a bio. You can set your 
  visibility to Public, Private, or Invisible.

* Blocking Chatters:
  If another chatter is bothering you, open their profile card and click "Block" 
  to hide their messages from your feed.

* Posting to the Member Feed:
  Once you have secured your username with a passkey, you can post updates 
  directly to the green "Registered Members Notices" column on the noticeboard.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DJ / PRESENTER INSTRUCTIONS (Level 1+)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Important Advice:
  If you are not sure how to use these commands, please ask management first. 
  Typing the wrong command can disrupt the station schedule.

* Command Format Guidelines:
  - Dates: 6-digit format with no spaces or slashes. E.g., July 10th 2026 is 100726.
  - Times: 4-digit 24-hour format. E.g., 8:00 PM is 2000.
  - DJ Names: If specifying another presenter, separate it at the end with a space.

* Going On-Air (Live):
  - Type: /show live
  - What it does: Updates the broadcast banner to show your name and logo live on air.

* Going Off-Air (Autopilot):
  - Type: /show tellstream
  - What it does: Resets the stream banner to autopilot when you finish your broadcast. 
    Only run this if you are the last presenter of the day.

* Scheduling a One-Off Show:
  - Type: /schedule temp Date StartTime EndTime TimeZone DJName
  - Example: /schedule temp 100726 2000 2200 BST Cruss
  - What it does: Adds a temporary single show slot to the calendar.

* Posting to DJ Noticeboards:
  DJs/Presenters can post noticeboard updates in 2 columns: "Selectors" and 
  "Fambily" (Registered Members). Specify which column before submitting.

* Word Filter Moderation:
  - Type: /add word       - Adds a bad word to the blocked list.
  - Type: /del word       - Removes a word from the blocked list.
  - Type: /listwords      - Lists all currently blocked words.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STATION ADMIN INSTRUCTIONS (Level 2+)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Station Admin Control:
  Station Admins have full control over the website. They can upload logos, 
  manage event flyers, promote users, edit the filter, and unban players.

* Adding a Repeating Weekly Slot:
  - Type: /schedule perm DayOfWeek StartTime EndTime TimeZone DJName
  - Example: /schedule perm Friday 2000 2200 BST Cruss
  - What it does: Sets a repeating weekly show slot in the schedule.

* Cancelling a Scheduled Show:
  - Type: /schedule cancel Date StartTime
  - Example: /schedule cancel 100726 2000
  - What it does: Removes a permanent or temporary show from the schedule.

* Posting to the Boss Noticeboard:
  Station Admins can post updates directly to the red "Boss Notices" column.

* Presenter Logo Management:
  - Type: /upload PresenterName   - Opens a window to upload a transparent PNG logo.
  - Type: /delete PresenterName   - Removes a presenter's logo from storage.

* Event Flyer Management:
  - Type: /uploadflyer Date FlyerName - Opens a window to upload a flyer image. 
                                        Note: The file name on your computer must 
                                        begin with the 6-digit date followed by a 
                                        space, e.g., 100726 SummerBash.jpg
  - Type: /deleteflyer Date FlyerName - Removes a flyer from storage.

* User Role Management:
  - Type: /setlevel username LevelNumber - Sets the access level (0 = Listener, 
                                           1 = DJ, 2 = Admin).

* VIP Status Management:
  - Type: /promote VIP DurationInMonths username (e.g., /promote VIP 3 username, 
                                                  or use the word perm for permanent)
  - Type: /demote VIP username                  - Removes VIP status.

* Unbanning Users:
  - Type: /unban username
  - What it does: Clears all strikes and restores chat/noticeboard access 
    instantly for locked or banned users.
